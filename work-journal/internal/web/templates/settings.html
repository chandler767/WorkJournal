General

Screen capture

Screenshots are taken when you switch apps or windows and when the screen changes, at most one per minimum gap, and at least one per idle backstop. Each change applies after Restart recorder (below).

Audio

Auto keeps the last two minutes of audio in memory only and starts recording, from the start of that buffer, when another app uses the microphone, a meeting app is in front, or a back-and-forth conversation is heard nearby. It stops three minutes after the call or conversation ends. Screenshots are not affected. The menu bar and the day page switch modes too.

Privacy

Before anything is summarized or indexed, Claude checks each hour's speech. Conversations near the laptop that you are not part of, and your personal non-work conversations with people who are not coworkers (a family call, say), are removed from transcripts, search and notes; talk with coworkers is kept. When unsure, speech is kept. Raw audio is unchanged and is deleted with the rest of the day's raw media.

Claude usage

When Claude's usage limit is reached, nothing more is sent to Claude until it resets; recording and local transcription go on, and the waiting hours are summarized as soon as the limit resets. The day page says so and offers "Run now anyway". Off: Claude is asked again on the usual schedule.

Exclusions

One per line. Exclusion changes take effect after the recorder restarts.

Categories and rules

Recorder

Restarting applies the current exclusions and screen capture settings; the supervisor starts it again with new arguments.
